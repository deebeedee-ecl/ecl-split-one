About ECL Hub

Your ECL profile, inhouse records and local League collector.

On matchday

  1. Connect your ECL account

    Use your existing ECL email and password. Remember me saves an encrypted session, never your password. Website links open your default browser.

  2. Open League

    Keep League and ECL Hub running on the same Windows PC. No WeGame cookie is needed.

  3. Collect your match

    Enter a Game ID or opt into automatic collection. The latest five games are checked once a minute, including non-ECL games. Failed requests are queued.

  4. Keep the result

    Saved local games are separate from official Inhouse History. The server must verify the complete roster and teams before any game counts.

KOOK reporting pilot

Requires server deployment and explicit session consent in Collector. Only assigned requests are uploaded for administrator review. League-account ownership is not independently proven; no automatic scoring is enabled.

PRIVACY

Match data, not your personal life.

No added analytics, advertising or activity tracking. Match uploads are off until you connect the reporting pilot and approve the session consent prompt.

What it reads
Your signed-in ECL profile, own inhouse records and saved Lzyumi League snapshot, plus public news, events and standings. The local collector reads League connection details and requested matches, including player names, PUUIDs, champions and statistics.
What it does not access
Your keystrokes, personal messages, browser history, contacts, microphone, camera or unrelated personal files. It does not scan process memory, inject code or modify League files.
Where match data goes
Raw matches and the collection queue are saved on this PC. If you connect the reporting pilot, your League name, tag and PUUID are sent to https://eclchina.lol to bind the session. Requested match JSON, including all ten players' names, PUUIDs and statistics, is uploaded and stored there for administrator review. Other local matches and the sample preview are not uploaded. League may contact Tencent for history; website requests expose normal connection information such as your IP address.
Passwords and login
ECL credentials go only to the existing Supabase authentication service over HTTPS. Passwords are never saved. Remember me uses Windows-encrypted session storage; otherwise the session lasts until Quit. League tokens stay in main-process memory and are used only on 127.0.0.1. Browser login is separate.
Raw match retention
Local raw matches expire after 30 days and are removed when Hub runs. Server uploads have a 30-day retention policy; scheduled server cleanup must be enabled before the reporting pilot launches. Approved results and minimal duplicate-check identifiers are kept separately.
League profile refresh
When your saved snapshot is stale, Hub asks the ECL website to refresh available League ranks, champions and recent statistics through Lzyumi. Your ECL password and local League credentials are not sent to Lzyumi.
Your controls
Automatic collection and reporting are separate opt-ins. Disabling collection stops discovery, not already queued work. Disconnect reporting, sign out or Quit stops future uploads; already received server data is not retracted. Closing the window only hides to the tray. Delete local files with the app closed; ask ECL administrators about stored report review data.
A clear distinction
In-game names and player identifiers can identify people, so we do not claim that the app processes zero personal data. The live Hub still uses the account and profile information you provide, and normal website actions remain live. No new collector uploads will be enabled without a separate, clear explanation and consent step.
