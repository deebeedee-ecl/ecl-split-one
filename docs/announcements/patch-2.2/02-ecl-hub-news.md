# Meet ECL Hub — your ECL desktop app

We’re bringing ECL to your desktop with **ECL Hub for Windows**: one place for
your profile, ranked ladder, inhouse scoreboards and saved League history.

Use your existing ECL login, choose English or Chinese, and keep Hub alongside
League while you play. You can inspect match statistics, view recorded awards,
check saved League snapshots and collect completed games on your own PC.

The Windows installer will be pinned in **ECL HUB DESKTOP APP** on KOOK
(channel **5590769063605712**). The first tester package is **1.0.1**; **Patch
2.2** is the wider ECL community update, not the desktop app's version number.

## A simpler reporting flow

The upcoming reporting pilot connects KOOK to your desktop collector:

1. Organise the inhouse with **!inhouse** and **!ready**.
2. Play with the ten verified players on their assigned sides.
3. The participating Hub reporter sends **!report IH_NUMBER GAME_ID**.
4. Their connected Hub collects the assigned game and uploads it for
   administrator review.

Only the designated reporter needs collection running. All ten players still
need verified ECL profiles. Reporting must be explicitly connected in Collector
and enabled for the reporter’s profile.

**Saved locally does not mean reported. Uploaded does not mean scored.**
Uploads and review receipts do not automatically create official results or
award LP. We’ll announce when the reporting pilot is enabled.
