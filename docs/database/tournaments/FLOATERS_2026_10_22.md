# Split II: 25 main players and separate floaters

Requested October 22, 2026. Preserve all 25 existing registrations, identifiers,
signup ordering, profile snapshots and accepted consent evidence. Main capacity
is now 25. Floaters have a separate list and do not consume main places.

The owner confirmed: a floater may play **one tournament match per team** across
Split II; scrims do not count. The signup records consent to this rule. This
release does not record match appearances or automatically reject a second
appearance: admins must track usage until the tournament lineup/reporting flow
is connected. No desktop, bot, inhouse scoring or old event data is changed.

## Storage and checks

- Existing tournament registrations gain a checked pool: MAIN (default) or FLOATER.
  The same event/profile and event/League-identity unique constraints cover both
  pools. Registration pool and accepted snapshots cannot be edited after signup.
- The event gains independent floaters_open and floater_rules_version fields.
  Floaters can be open while main registration is closed, and while play is in
  progress. Completed/archived events reject new floater registrations.
- Server-side verified/active profile checks, profile-derived role/identity,
  cross-pool duplicate checks, privacy and explicit consent apply to floaters.
- Existing consent is retained under split-ii-registration-2026-10-16. Its wording
  is available as historical rules. New main rules and floater rules have separate
  October 22 versions; existing players are not silently re-consented.
